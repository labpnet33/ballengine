One set of settings, everywhere

The player, the API and Claude read the same settings with the same names. Design a look once and your website and your rendered videos match.

On your website

<iframe src="https://ballengine.app/embed?key=be_pk_…
  &mode=ESCAPE&theme=gold_luxury
  &title_text=Wait for it&speed=1.5"></iframe>

// or change it live from your page:
player.contentWindow.postMessage({
  target: 'ballengine', type: 'set-settings',
  settings: { theme: 'gold_luxury', title_text: 'Wait for it' }
}, 'https://ballengine.app');

In a cloud render (or through Claude)

POST https://ballengine.app/api/v1/videos
Authorization: Bearer be_sk_…

{
  "mode": "ESCAPE",
  "theme": "gold_luxury",
  "title_text": "Wait for it",
  "speed": 1.5,
  "duration": 30,
  "aspect_ratio": "9:16"
}
SettingWhat it does
modeWhich simulation: FLAG_BATTLE, RHYTHMIC, ESCAPE, SPACE_SPIRAL and more
themeA finished colour look, such as cyberpunk_neon or gold_luxury
paletteYour own colours instead of a theme, up to 8
title_textThe headline drawn on the video
watermark_textYour handle or brand on the video
speedSimulation speed, 0.5 to 3
ball_countHow many balls (or flags in Flag Battle)
gravityGravity, 0 to 5
sound_effectThe bounce sound
config_overridesFiner settings: ball colour, trails, backdrop, title colour and more

Only a cloud render takes duration, aspect_ratio, quality and music. The player makes 9:16 videos of 5 to 60 seconds on the visitor's device.

Questions

What can I build with BallEngine?
A ball simulator website, a video tool, a bot or an app. Show the live simulator on your site with one iframe, render finished MP4s from your server with one API call, or let Claude and other AI agents make videos through our hosted MCP server. All three use the same engine and the same settings.
Why not build my own ball simulator with AI?
You can, but you start from zero: physics, recording, audio sync and every browser bug are yours. BallEngine is a finished engine with every mode, built and kept working by game developers with 10+ years of experience.
Do the player and the API look the same?
Yes. Both read the same settings with the same names (theme, palette, title_text, speed and the rest), through the same code, so a look you design for your site renders the same in the cloud.
Who pays for the videos?
Downloads from the website player are made on your visitors' own devices and use no credits; they count toward your site's monthly videos. Cloud renders use your cloud credits, from your plan or bought: from the API, Claude, the Studio, or the Cloud video button in your player. 1 credit is 30 seconds at 720p, 2 at 1080p 60 fps.
Can my visitors make cloud videos, like on BallEngine?
Yes. Switch on Cloud videos for your player in your console and it gets a Cloud video button: an HD video made on our GPUs, paid from your plan's cloud credits. You set how many credits a month the site may spend, how many videos each visitor gets and the longest length. To give them only to your paying users, require a one-time token that your server makes with your secret key.
Can Claude set it up for me?
Yes. Your console has a Copy AI prompt button with your keys and websites filled in. Paste it into Claude, ChatGPT or Cursor in your project and the assistant adds the player, and the API if you want it.
Who can use it?
Anyone can read how it works. Access is invite-only for now: pick the plan you would want and press Confirm interest, and we invite people in order.
