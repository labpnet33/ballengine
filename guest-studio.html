Mode behavior

Escape creates rotating rings. Rhythmic follows a zigzag path. Spawner uses a bounded bouncing-ball arena. More original mode algorithms are not yet implemented.

Physics & Dynamics

Gravity and bounce are applied in Spawner mode. Speed is controlled under Basics.

Sound, MIDI & Audio

Audio stays on your device. Beat energy can influence the animation and imported audio can be included in exported clips. A built-in music catalog and MIDI editor are not available.

Recording & Export

Unlimited local WebM recording with optional imported audio and no watermark, login, credits or server upload. 1080p and 60 fps require more device performance. If the recording stutters, use 720p and 30 fps.

Save system

Guest scenes are saved in this browser only. No account or cloud service required.

Export / Import

Publishing

A guest session does not connect your YouTube, Instagram, or TikTok accounts. Export a video locally and upload it using your own account.

Using the Studio

1. Open Basics and pick a scene.

2. Tune the physics and Arts settings.

3. Optionally import local music under Audio.

4. Press Record, then save your WebM.

5. Save a scene to your browser under Cloud.
